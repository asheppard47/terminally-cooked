# Research and Evidence

**This section holds the evidence behind the decisions: external critique of the design and audience research into what this community actually finds funny.** Decisions themselves live in [product/decision-log.md](../product/decision-log.md) — this is the material those rulings were made from, kept separate so a ruling can be re-examined against what was actually known.

## Audience research

A 2026-08-18 X sweep of AI-coding-CLI culture shaped the catalog. Per-claim post URLs live in private workshop notes, not this repo. Headlines that shaped this product:

- **Mocked**: streak-chasing, contribution graphs as proof of skill, and leaderboards. One reported case of an internal AI-coding leaderboard producing farmed busywork before being scrapped.
- **Embraced**: Wrapped-style shareable recap cards and low-stakes joke achievements, shared unironically.
- **Vocabulary**: "YOLO mode" and "let it cook" cleared the bar for recognition without explanation and became catalog entries.
- **Shared artifacts**: token and cost dashboards are the one thing this audience demonstrably screenshots, in a mixed brag-and-complaint register — the same register as the card.

## Evidence standards used here

Claims are recorded with their source and their weakness. The X angles that returned thin results are labeled thin rather than dressed up; a reviewer finding that was rejected is recorded with the reason. Two review legs failed outright on infrastructure limits and are reported as unavailable rather than quietly replaced by a substitute reviewer.
